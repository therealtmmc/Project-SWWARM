Key Operational Simulation Behavior

Scenario 5

Tri-Hybrid Storm Advantage

During severe weather, solar output drops to zero, but wind output rises to 80% of rating (160 Wh/hr) and wave output doubles to 60 Wh/hr. In Emergency Survival Mode the platform generates a net surplus of +214.0 Wh/hr, recharging a depleted battery from 20% to 100% SoC in 17.9 hours in the middle of a typhoon.

Scenario 4

Overcast Resiliency

On heavy overcast days with low wind, the ETFE monocrystalline solar deck produces 40% output (102 Wh/hr). Combined with low wind and wave inputs, the platform still achieves a net positive balance of +41.7 Wh/hr, allowing full Active Sampling Mode without draining battery reserves.

Scenarios 7 - 9

Doldrums Discharge Rates

With zero wind and a glassy sea at night, the usable battery bank (3,840 Wh, 100% to the 20% low-power threshold) lasts:

  • Active Sampling: 36.5 hours
  • Baseline Mode: 462.7 hours (19.3 days)
  • Emergency Survival: 640.0 hours (26.7 days)
