Key Operational Simulation Behavior

Scenario 5

Tri-Hybrid Storm Scenario [SIMULATION]

During severe-weather simulation, solar output is set to zero while wind and wave generation are modeled at 160 W and 80 Wh/hr respectively. In Emergency Survival Mode, the modeled net balance is +234.0 Wh/hr. These environmental-generation values are assumptions and require turbine/wave-converter power curves and metocean validation. [REF-02]

Scenario 4

Overcast Resiliency [SIMULATION]

Under the current overcast simulation, solar yield is modeled at 102 Wh/hr (40% of the 255 Wh/hr scenario input). Combined with modeled low-wind and wave inputs, the net balance is +59.8 Wh/hr. This is a simulation assumption, not a panel datasheet output. [REF-01]

Scenarios 7 - 9

Doldrums Discharge Rates

With zero wind and a glassy sea at night, the 4,178 Wh usable design capacity is modeled to last: [REF-02]

  • Active Sampling: 39.7 hours
  • Baseline Mode: 503.4 hours (21.0 days)
  • Emergency Survival: 696.3 hours (29.0 days)
Duration calculations use the project's 4,178 Wh usable-energy design assumption. [CALC]
